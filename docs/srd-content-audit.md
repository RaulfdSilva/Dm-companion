# SRD 5.2.1 treasure content and licensing audit

Research date: 2026-11-07. Scope: the official English SRD 5.2.1 and CC BY 4.0.
This document proposes no approved product scope and implements no application data or features.

**Evidence labels:** **Verified** means checked against the identified primary source;
**Interpretation** is an assessment of those sources; **Recommendation** is a proposal
for owner review; **Unresolved** identifies a question this audit does not settle.

## Executive summary

- **Verified:** Wizards of the Coast licenses the contents of SRD 5.2.1 under CC BY 4.0.
  Redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use, are permitted subject to
  its conditions. This applies to the identified SRD document, not the entire D&D
  catalog. See [Legal Information, p. 1][srd1] and [CC sections 2–3][legal].
- **Verified:** Reusable treasure ingredients include five currencies, equipment
  values and resale guidance, a random Trinkets table, magic-item categories and
  values, and the complete entries in the SRD's Magic Items A–Z section. The
  inventory below gives page and section references.
- **Verified:** SRD 5.2.1 contains **neither individual-treasure nor treasure-hoard
  generation tables organized by Challenge Rating (CR)**. It also lacks general
  gemstone/art-object selection tables and the DMG's themed random-magic-item
  tables. Included item-effect tables are not replacements for loot distributions.
  See the availability review and its whole-document search method below.
- **Interpretation:** An SRD-only implementation cannot claim to reproduce the
  official 2024 DMG treasure generator from this license alone. It needs additional
  distribution data with an established rights basis, or independently authored
  distributions clearly identified as project content.
- **Recommendation:** The owner should choose between a small original generator
  using verified SRD ingredients, a reduced tool without automatic CR-based
  distributions, or a separately cleared content source. No option is selected here.

## Availability of individual and hoard treasure tables

**Verified answer: neither type is present in SRD 5.2.1, whether CR-indexed or
otherwise presented as a general treasure-generation table.**

This conclusion comes from reviewing the Contents ([pp. 2–4][srd2]), the relevant
Equipment and Magic Items sections, and searching the entire extracted PDF text.
Case-insensitive searches after whitespace normalization found no occurrences of
“individual treasure,” “treasure hoard,” “random treasure,” “treasure themes,” or
“random magic items.” Inspection of the actual tables establishes the distinction
between valuation, item effects, starting equipment, and loot generation; the
finding does not rely on a failed keyword search alone.

| Requested generator input | SRD finding | Related official material and verification boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual treasure by CR | Absent. No licensed purse amounts or per-monster distribution was found. | The DMG's Chapter 4 Adventure Rewards is listed in its [official contents][dmg]. The paid chapter redirected to a purchase page during this audit, so its specific individual-treasure rows and CR bands were not independently verified. Do not treat third-party reproductions as evidence of CC coverage. |
| Treasure hoards by CR | Absent. No licensed hoard amounts, CR bands, or reward probabilities was found. | The [official errata][errata] confirms the DMG's Random Treasure Hoard table under Chapter 4, Treasure Hoards, p. 121. The full table was not accessible for row-by-row verification. Its existence does not place it in the SRD. |
| Themed random magic-item selection | Absent. Catalog entries and rarity values are present, but selection weights are not. | DMG Chapter 7 → Random Magic Items lists Arcana, Armaments, Implements, and Relics tables in the [official contents][dmg]. These are not the SRD's nine item categories. |
| General gemstone/art-object catalogs and selection tables | Absent. The selling rule mentions valuables, but no such catalogs/tables are supplied. | Gemstones and Art Objects are separate sections of DMG Chapter 7 in the [official contents][dmg]. Their detailed values, descriptions, and distributions were not cleared by this audit. |
| Magic-item award frequency by level/CR | No general award schedule found. The higher-level starting-equipment table is a different rule. | DMG Chapter 7 includes Awarding Magic Items in the [official contents][dmg]. Do not infer an award schedule from rarity or starting-equipment quantities. |

The CR-based tables actually present in Monsters → Challenge Rating,
[p. 256][srd256], concern XP and proficiency, not treasure. Gameplay Toolbox →
Combat Encounters, pp. 202–203, concerns encounter difficulty, not a conversion
from XP into money. No such conversion should be labeled an SRD rule.

## Missing content and licensing limitations

### Data still needed for an automatic treasure generator

**Interpretation, based on the verified absences above:** the SRD does not settle
coin quantities, individual-versus-hoard scaling, CR bands for treasure, rarity
chances, magic-item selection weights, or generic gem/art descriptions and prices.
These need a separate source or original design if the owner wants those features.
Nor does the SRD determine a project-specific reward balance or output format.

**Verified distinction:** some DMG material is also included in the SRD, such as
specific magic-item entries and valuation rules. It is incorrect to exclude all
DMG-derived content categorically. Conversely, a DMG section, item, table, or web
page absent from this SRD has **no redistribution permission established by this
SRD's license**. “Outside the SRD” here does not assert exclusivity across every
publication or every possible license.

The [Creator FAQ][faq], in its Basic Rules question, explicitly says the D&D Beyond
Basic Rules and SRD are different resources and only the SRD is being released
under Creative Commons. Free access, a purchased book, an SRD-related search label,
or an online citation is not evidence of permission to redistribute additional
content. The FAQ's guidance on referencing non-SRD books is citation guidance,
not a license to copy their tables.

**Interpretation:** lack of a CC grant is not a claim that every game mechanic,
number, or generic concept is protected by copyright. [CC section 2(a)(2)][legal]
preserves applicable exceptions and limitations. Whether a particular non-SRD
reuse is independently lawful requires a separate assessment; this audit does not
clear it. Renaming or rearranging a copied table does not establish independent
authorship or expand the SRD grant.

### Attribution and downstream use

**Verified obligations:** [SRD Legal Information, p. 1][srd1] supplies the following
notice. It is reproduced here for attribution, not as application implementation:

> This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 (“SRD 5.2.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

SRD content in this audit is summarized and reorganized; the proposed schema and
recommendations are not Wizards-authored rules. The SRD's notice points to the
disclaimer of warranties and limitation of liability in [CC section 5][legal].

For a later distribution, [CC section 3(a)][legal] requires retaining supplied
creator/attribution information, copyright notices if supplied, license and
disclaimer notices, and a reasonably practicable source link; indicating changes
and retaining earlier change indications; and including the license text or link.
The medium may determine a reasonable presentation. The SRD asks that no other
attribution to Wizards or its parent/affiliates be added beyond its supplied notice.

**Recommendation:** carry the notice, source/version, and a concrete change note
with any distributed data, and provide accessible in-app legal credits when SRD
data is shipped. A repository-only README should not be assumed sufficient for a
standalone app download. Exact placement remains for owner review; no UI is added here.

[CC section 2(a)(5)(B)][legal] prohibits additional terms or effective technological
measures that restrict recipients' licensed rights in the SRD material.
**Interpretation:** CC BY is not a requirement to release the whole application's
source code or license all original code under CC BY. Nevertheless, a later EULA,
data packaging, or app-store distribution must preserve recipients' CC rights in
the included material. Original code/content licensing remains an owner decision;
the SRD license cannot simply be replaced with the project's code license. See
also section 3(a)(4) on adapted material and section 4 on applicable database rights.

### Trademarks and endorsement

**Verified:** [CC section 2(b)(2)][legal] does not license trademark rights, and
section 2(a)(6) grants no right to imply sponsorship, endorsement, connection, or
official status. SRD p. 1 expressly permits the compatibility phrases
“compatible with fifth edition” and “5E compatible.”

**Recommendation:** use the SRD's compatibility wording if compatibility marketing
is desired; do not assume this license permits D&D/Wizards logos, a dragon
ampersand, or branding that suggests an official product. Use of D&D marks in
names or marketing would need separate review of that particular use. This is not
a finding that all descriptive reference to D&D is forbidden. No project name,
store description, or branding decision is approved by this audit.

## Proposed minimal data structure for treasure tables

**Recommendation only:** a small data model, to be reviewed before implementation.
No populated treasure rows, probabilities, prices for non-SRD valuables, or
generation logic are supplied here. JSON versus TypeScript is not decided.

| Record | Proposed fields and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Source | `id`, `title`, `version`, `url`, `license`, `attribution`, `changes`. Original project content must identify its author and separately chosen license; an SRD label is not a default for new distributions. |
| Table | `id`, `version`, `kind` (individual or hoard), optional `crMin`/`crMax`, `selectorDice`, `rows`, and `sourceId`. No CR bands are prefilled or attributed to the SRD. |
| Row | Inclusive `min`/`max` selector bounds, `rewards`, and a source override if the row has a different origin. Range width expresses probability without a second weight system. |
| Reward | Discriminated `kind` (coin, valuable, or magic item), an amount, and either a currency code, valuable reference, or stable item ID. No unverified item can enter the catalog merely because a row references it. |
| Amount | Either a fixed nonnegative integer or a dice expression with an optional positive integer multiplier. Keep multiplication separate from the existing dice engine API. |
| Catalog item | Stable `id`, name, kind/category, optional rarity/attunement/variant, source section/page, and `sourceId`. Store a numeric value only when supported; “priceless” or unknown must not become zero. |

Use integer copper units for numeric valuations and explicit CP/SP/EP/GP/PP codes
for coin quantities; this is a proposed accounting representation, not a new SRD
rule. Keep an original table's provenance separate from the SRD item records it
references. Proposed validation includes complete, non-overlapping selector ranges,
resolved item/source IDs, supported dice expressions, and safe integer arithmetic.
The schema does not supply missing rules or establish rights to populate a table.

## Recommendations for implementing the v0.1 treasure generator

These are options and implementation gates for owner review, not approved work:

1. **Choose the source strategy.** If the owner wants SRD-only licensed ingredients,
   consider independently authored distributions and label those distributions as
   project rules. Alternatively, defer automatic distributions or investigate a
   separately licensed source. Do not promise official DMG-equivalent results.
2. **Consider a smaller first release.** Coins plus an explicitly selected subset
   of SRD items could reduce transcription and review work. Trinkets are another
   licensed option for flavor. Whether to include magic items, gems/art, artifacts,
   or CR selection remains undecided; defer unsupported catalogs if not authored
   and reviewed separately.
3. **Audit records before bundling them.** For each proposed item/variant, record
   its exact SRD page, rarity, requirements, valuation exceptions, and changes.
   A spell scroll's spell content also needs its own verified source. Do not import
   commercial-book names, images, lore, or tooltips by association.
4. **Keep selection design separate from item rules.** Independently choose and
   review any CR scaling, hoard size, rarity chances, and item weights. Record that
   authorship; do not present these choices as extracted SRD tables or reskin a
   copied DMG distribution. Balance is unverified until a later design/test task.
5. **Reuse the dice engine through its existing API.** If approved tables require
   scaling a roll, apply that in a later generator layer. This document changes
   neither the dice engine nor the expressions it accepts.
6. **Complete attribution and distribution review before release.** Preserve the
   required notice and change history with the data; review proposed branding,
   credits placement, and distribution terms. Add data-validation and deterministic
   generator tests in a later implementation task, after content is approved.

### Unresolved questions for the project owner

- Is v0.1 intended to reproduce official DMG output, or are clearly labeled original
  distributions acceptable? The former is not cleared by this SRD audit.
- Is CR selection required in v0.1, and who will author and review its balance if
  original tables are chosen?
- Which reward kinds and SRD item subset are wanted? How should unnamed valuables
  or artifacts be handled if those are later approved?
- What licenses will cover original project tables and code, and what distribution
  channels and branding will be used?
- If official non-SRD material remains a requirement, what additional rights basis
  will be established? Detailed 2024 DMG individual/hoard rows and CR bands remain
  unverified here because the full chapters were not accessible. No third-party
  reproduction was used to fill that gap.

## Validation and scope check

- Verified the official SRD download/version, PDF hash, 26 page/heading references,
  33 citation labels, and the cited legal-code sections. All seven unique cited
  URLs returned HTTP 200 at their expected destinations; page fragments reference
  pages in the same downloaded PDF.
- Reviewed the absence findings using both full-document normalized searches and
  relevant section/table inspection. Distinguished generic loot distributions from
  included trinket and item-effect tables.
- Checked claims against the license grant, attribution conditions, no-endorsement
  clause, and trademark exclusion. Recommendations and legal interpretations are
  labeled; access to a website is never treated as a content license.
- The DMG contents and errata establish only the specifically identified book
  sections/table existence. Their publication is not treated as a CC grant.
- Only `docs/srd-content-audit.md` is added. No application code, dice engine, UI,
  dependencies, treasure datasets, or generation logic are changed. Runtime tests
  are not applicable to this documentation-only task.
